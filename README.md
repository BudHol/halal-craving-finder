# HalalBites HK

HalalBites HK is a Hong Kong halal food discovery experience that helps users
find restaurants based on what they want to eat, how much they want to spend,
and where they want to go.

## Features

### Discover halal restaurants

- Explore **199 restaurants** across Hong Kong.
- Browse restaurant cards with cuisine, neighborhood, price range, halal
  status, and featured menu items.
- View restaurants across Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, and the New Territories
  and Outlying Islands.
- See curated food imagery based on restaurant cuisine and craving categories.

### Search by craving

- Search by:
  - Restaurant name
  - Cuisine
  - Menu item
  - Food craving
- Use natural food-related terms such as:
  - `shawarma`, `doner`, or `kebab`
  - `curry`, `biryani`, or `tandoori`
  - `dumpling`, `dim sum`, or `Chinese`
  - `burger`, `fried chicken`, or `wings`
- Search recognizes related terms and minor spelling differences to make
  discovering food easier.

### Browse by food category

Filter restaurants by craving category:

- Kebabs & Shawarma
- Dim Sum & Chinese
- Curries & Biryani
- Noodles & Soups
- Burgers & Chicken
- Hot Pot
- Middle Eastern
- Pizza & Steaks
- Southeast Asian

### Filter by budget

Choose a price range that fits your plans:

- Up to HK$80
- Up to HK$150
- Up to HK$300
- HK$500 and above

### Filter by location

- Switch between all regions, Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, and the New
  Territories and Outlying Islands.
- Select a specific neighborhood.
- See the number of restaurants available in each neighborhood.
- Combine location filters with craving, search, and budget filters.

### Restaurant details

Open any restaurant to view:

- Restaurant name
- Cuisine
- Address
- Neighborhood and region
- Telephone number
- Price range and price tier
- Menu highlights
- Halal classification
- Certificate expiry information
- Evidence status

### Halal information

The app explains the halal classifications used in the restaurant catalog:

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `HK` | Halal-certified kitchen with segregated preparation and certification |
| `H` | Halal-certified restaurant |
| `HF` | Halal-friendly restaurant or restaurant with halal options/counters |

The in-app **Halal Guide** provides additional context for these labels.

### Google Maps links

Each restaurant includes a direct Google Maps link, making it easy to check
the location and plan a visit.

### Surprise Me

Use **Surprise Me** to randomly select a restaurant from the current results.
The selection respects active craving, search, location, and budget filters.

### Light and dark themes

- Switch between light and dark themes.
- The interface follows the device's preferred color scheme when no preference
  has been selected.
- Theme preference is remembered for future visits.

### Responsive interface

- Designed for mobile and desktop screens.
- Responsive restaurant grid and search controls.
- Touch-friendly filter chips, buttons, cards, and modal dialogs.
- Smooth scrolling, transitions, and interactive hover states.

### Convenient interactions

- Results update as filters and search terms change.
- The **Find Food** action takes users directly to the results.
- Clear search input with one click.
- Reset all active filters from the empty-results state.
- Close dialogs with a close button, backdrop click, or the `Escape` key.

## User experience

HalalBites HK is designed to make restaurant discovery quick and focused:

1. Choose a craving or search for a dish.
2. Narrow the results by budget or location.
3. Open a restaurant for its full details.
4. Follow the Google Maps link to check the location.
5. Use **Surprise Me** when you want a recommendation.

Restaurant information, prices, opening hours, menus, and certification status
can change. Users should confirm current details with the restaurant before
visiting.
