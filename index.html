Hong Kong Halal Certification Guide

Understanding official certificates issued in Hong Kong

H 100% Halal Certified Restaurant

Certified dining establishment where all meats, ingredients, and kitchens comply strictly with Halal guidelines.

HK Halal Certified Kitchen

Dedicated segregated Halal kitchen with certified supplies, storage, and utensils.

HF Halal Friendly / Certified Meat Supply

Dedicated Halal options, verified certified meat suppliers, or Muslim-friendly menus.
